    Our Story

    Cleaning beyond expectations, one order at a time

    CleanArk started with a simple idea: laundry day shouldn't take up your day. We built a doorstep pickup-and-delivery service so you can hand off wash & fold, wash & iron, dry cleaning and steam pressing without ever leaving home.

    Every order is sorted by fabric type and handled with the right process — gentle wash cycles for everyday cottons, careful dry cleaning for suits and sarees, and precise steam pressing for a crisp, ready-to-wear finish.
